# The Continuity Bureau

The landing site and manifesto of **The Continuity Bureau** — a founding-phase
political movement for the United States and Canada, built on decentralization,
subsidiarity, and *verify, don't trust*. A personal political project of
**Miles Droolhouse**.

**Non-affiliation:** The Continuity Bureau is a personal project of Miles
Droolhouse. It is not a product of, or affiliated with, Drooly Inc., ICEFAM
Records, or any company.

## Status (honest, per Manifesto §11)

The Continuity Bureau is in its founding phase. It is not, at this time, a
registered political party in any state of the United States. It is not, at
this time, a registered political party with Elections Canada or any provincial
elections authority. No candidates. No ballot lines. No electoral track record.
Registration in relevant jurisdictions is administrative work in progress, and
it will take the time honest paperwork takes.
